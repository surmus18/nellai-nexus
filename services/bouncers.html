Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about booking bouncers & event security in Tirunelveli.

How many bouncers should I book for a wedding reception in Tirunelveli?

For weddings with 300 to 500 guests, 2 to 4 bouncers (1 pair at the main entrance gate and 1 pair near the stage dais) are ideal. For grand receptions with 1000+ guests or celebrity/VIP attendees, a squad of 6 to 8 bouncers with a team lead ensures total crowd discipline and smooth bride-groom stage entry.

What uniform do the Nellai Nexus bouncers wear?

We offer two standard professional attire choices: 1) Formal All-Black Safari Suit (prestigious & traditional for wedding dais) and 2) Tactical All-Black Polo T-shirt & Cargo Pants with security badges. Formal corporate blazers are also available for summits and award functions upon advance request.

Are your security bouncers verified and trained in polite de-escalation?

Yes, 100%. Every bouncer in our roster is strictly background-verified with government ID proof, physically fit (minimum 5'11" to 6'2"), and professionally trained to handle enthusiastic crowds politely without causing embarrassment or friction to family relatives.

What time will the bouncers report to the marriage hall?

Our security personnel report to the venue 60 minutes prior to your event start time. The team supervisor conducts an immediate entrance perimeter check, liaises with family members or event organizers, and secures all dais access points before the first guests arrive.

Do your bouncers assist with VIP and bride-groom stage entry escorts?

Yes, absolutely! Escorting the bride and groom during their grand entry walk through the crowd, creating a respectful protective pathway, and controlling selfie queues on the stage dais are standard core responsibilities included in all our packages.

How do I confirm the booking and what advance payment is required?

You can check date availability using our instant form or contact us via WhatsApp. A nominal 20% token advance confirms the booking and locks your security roster for the date. The remaining amount is settled after duty completion on event day.
