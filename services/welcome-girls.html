Frequently Asked Questions

How many welcome girls should I book for my wedding?

For events with 200â€“500 guests, 2 to 4 welcome girls is ideal. For larger marriage halls with multiple entry gates or VIP corridors, 6 to 8 hostesses ensure seamless greeting without guest crowding.

Do you bring the welcoming items like Aarti plates and Panneer sombu?

Yes! Our team arrives fully equipped with brass Aarti plates, camphor deepam, scented rose water (panneer sombu), fresh sandalwood (chandan) paste, sacred kumkum, and kalkandu crystals. You do not need to prepare anything extra.

Can we specify saree color themes to match our hall decor?

Yes, we can coordinate sarees (Traditional Maroon/Gold, Mustard Yellow, Pastel Peach, Mint Green) to match your wedding stage decor color palette with prior notice.

What time do the hostesses report at the venue?

Our hostesses arrive 1 hour (60 minutes) prior to your scheduled event start time, fully dressed in attire, with flowers and aarti ready to receive the very first guest.

Do you take orders for outside Tirunelveli (Tenkasi, Tuticorin)?

Yes, we regularly provide event hostesses across Tenkasi, Thoothukudi, Kovilpatti, Sankarankovil, and Ambasamudram. Safe vehicle pickup and drop is organized directly from our central Palayamkottai hub.

How to book and what is the advance payment?

You can fill the enquiry form on this page or WhatsApp us. A nominal 20% advance secures your date and team. The balance payment is made on event day after service completion.
