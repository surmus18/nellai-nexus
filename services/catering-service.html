Frequently Asked Questions

Can you provide service staff if we have our own cooks?

Yes! We frequently supply professional service teams, chaffing equipment, and cutlery for independent caterers.

How many stewards are provided for buffet service?

Usually 1 steward per live counter and 1 clearing staff per 40 guests ensures smooth flow without crowds.

Do the stewards wear uniforms?

All our service crew members wear neat, matching black/white uniforms with aprons and hairnets.
