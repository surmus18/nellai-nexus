# Service Page Standard Blueprint - Nellai Nexus

Every service page in `services/*.html` must adhere to this comprehensive, high-converting standard format.

## Standard Sections Hierarchy
1. **Top Announcement Strip & Contact**:
   - Address (Samathanapuram, Palayamkottai), Business hours, Direct Phone, WhatsApp link, Social links.
2. **Sticky Header & Mega Menu**:
   - Logo, Navigation with 12 services mega-menu dropdown, Rentals, About, Contact, and "Enquire Now" CTA.
3. **Breadcrumbs Navigation**:
   - Home > Event Services > [Service Name].
4. **Hero Header**:
   - Trust badge (e.g., Traditional Hospitality / Verified Crew / 250+ Events).
   - High-impact SEO H1 title with localized keyword (Tirunelveli, Palayamkottai).
   - Value proposition description.
   - Dual CTAs: "Check Date Availability" (scroll to form) + "Chat on WhatsApp".
   - Key highlights strip (e.g., 100% Punctual Guarantee, Authentic Equipment/Items Included, Multilingual Tamil/English).
5. **Visual Photo Gallery (5 High-Resolution Images)**:
   - 1 Main Featured Setup (aspect-video / 2-col span).
   - 4 Contextual photos representing diverse setups, rituals, attire, and scale.
   - Interactive Lightbox or full-size modal viewing support.
6. **Package Comparison Cards (3 Tiers)**:
   - Tier 1: Silver / Essential (e.g. 2 Crew / Intimate event).
   - Tier 2: Gold / Royal (e.g. 4 Crew - Most Popular with highlighted border & badge).
   - Tier 3: Platinum / Grand Scale (e.g. 6 to 12 Crew / Mega events & summits).
   - Inclusions list for each tier with clear deliverables & instant WhatsApp booking button.
7. **Detailed Rituals, Scope of Work & Etiquette**:
   - Step-by-step breakdown of how the service operates on event day.
   - Cultural significance for South Tamil Nadu weddings & celebrations.
8. **Attire / Equipment / Style Options**:
   - Showcase themes, color coordination, and material standards.
9. **How Booking Works (4 Steps)**:
   - 1: Check Date & Hall Availability.
   - 2: Choose Team Size & Custom Theme.
   - 3: Quick Confirmation & Token Advance.
   - 4: On-time Arrival 1 Hour Prior to Event.
10. **Local Service Coverage Area Tags**:
    - Tirunelveli, Palayamkottai, Vannarpettai, Tenkasi, Thoothukudi, Kovilpatti, Ambasamudram, Nanguneri.
11. **Real Local Client Testimonials / Social Proof**:
    - 3 Verified client reviews citing specific marriage halls / venues in Nellai.
12. **Interactive Availability & Booking Form**:
    - Name, Phone, Event Date, Guest Count, Venue, Custom Requirements.
    - Double integration: triggers `sendNellaiEnquiry()` Brevo email notification + opens pre-filled WhatsApp message with unique Reference ID.
13. **Comprehensive FAQ Section**:
    - 5 to 6 practical questions and clear answers.
14. **Cross-Service Recommendations**:
    - Link to complementary services (Stage Decoration, Bouncers, Catering, Rentals).
15. **Footer & Sticky Mobile Bar**:
    - Full footer with contact details, links, and copyright.
    - Mobile bottom sticky action bar: "Call +91 96262 72817" & "WhatsApp Quote".
16. **SEO & Structured Data**:
    - Service Schema + LocalBusiness Schema.
    - FAQPage Schema with all FAQ questions.
    - OpenGraph & Twitter tags.
