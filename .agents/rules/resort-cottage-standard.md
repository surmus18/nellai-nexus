# Resort & Cottage Booking Standard Blueprint - Nellai Nexus

Every stay, cottage, and resort booking page in `resorts/*.html` or `resorts.html` must adhere to this comprehensive, high-converting standard format.

## Standard Sections Hierarchy
1. **Top Announcement Strip & Quick Booking Helpline**:
   - Office Address (Palayamkottai, Tirunelveli), Booking desk hours (8:00 AM - 10:00 PM), Direct Call (+91 96262 72817), WhatsApp Helpline (+91 96262 72818).
2. **Sticky Header & Navigation**:
   - Logo, Main Navigation (Home, Event Services Mega-Menu, Resorts & Cottages, Rentals, About, Contact) and "Check Availability" CTA.
3. **Breadcrumbs Navigation**:
   - Home > Resorts & Cottages > [Destination / Category].
4. **Hero Header with Quick Date & Location Search Strip**:
   - Trust badge (e.g., Verified Properties / Best Rate Guarantee / 24/7 Local Caretaker Assistance).
   - High-impact SEO H1 with localized keywords (e.g., "Best Luxury Resorts & Private Cottages in Courtallam & All Over Tamil Nadu").
   - Value proposition description (curated private pool villas, tea estate bungalows, misty mountain cottages, family homestays).
   - Interactive quick search / booking strip: Destination, Check-in Date, Check-out Date, Guests, and "Find Stays" button.
5. **Destination / Category Filter Tabs**:
   - Filter by Region: Courtallam (Tenkasi), Kodaikanal, Ooty & Coonoor, Yercaud, Valparai, Mahabalipuram / ECR, Rameshwaram / Kanyakumari.
   - Filter by Experience: Private Pool Villas, Mountain View Cottages, Plantation / Estate Stays, Family / Group Homestays, Budget Nature Camps.
6. **Property Showcase Grid & Visual Cards**:
   - High-resolution imagery (Bedroom, Balcony view, Pool, Living area).
   - Property title, verified badge, location tag with distance from town.
   - Key Amenity Badges: Swimming Pool, Mountain / Forest View, Campfire & BBQ, In-house Home Chef, Free Wi-Fi, Pet Friendly, Car Parking.
   - Pricing display: Transparent per-night indicative tariff (Weekday vs Weekend).
   - Dual action CTAs: "Book on WhatsApp" (pre-filled with property title) & "View Details / Enquire".
7. **Curated Amenities & Guest Experiences**:
   - Bonfire night with music setup.
   - Authentic South Indian / Tamil home-cooked catering arrangements.
   - Sightseeing, local trekking & jeep safari assistance.
   - Event hosting capabilities (Birthday parties, reunions, bachelorettes, family get-togethers).
8. **Why Book With Nellai Nexus**:
   - 100% Verified properties (No fake photos, inspected personally).
   - Direct caretaker contact provided 24 hours before check-in.
   - Flexible cancellation & date reschedule options.
   - Local support based out of South Tamil Nadu.
9. **Booking Process (4 Simple Steps)**:
   - 1: Select Destination & Travel Dates.
   - 2: Choose Property or Request Recommendations based on budget.
   - 3: Instant Date Hold with nominal advance payment.
   - 4: Receive GPS Location, Caretaker Details & Seamless Check-in.
10. **Standard Stay Guidelines & House Rules**:
    - Check-in: 12:00 PM | Check-out: 11:00 AM.
    - Government ID proof mandatory for all adult guests.
    - Music / Loud noise cutoff time as per local eco-zone guidelines.
    - Alcohol & smoking policies as per property regulations.
11. **Real Guest Reviews & Testimonials**:
    - 3+ Verified reviews from families, couples, and corporate group travelers.
12. **Interactive Availability & Booking Enquiry Form**:
    - Fields: Full Name, WhatsApp Number, Destination / Stay Preference, Check-in Date, Check-out Date, Number of Adults & Kids, Special Requests (Campfire, BBQ, Extra Bed, Pool).
    - Double integration: triggers `sendNellaiEnquiry()` Brevo email notification + opens pre-filled WhatsApp message with unique reference ID (`NX-STAY-XXXX`).
13. **Comprehensive FAQ Section**:
    - 5 to 6 practical FAQs (Payment terms, food arrangements, early check-in, pet friendliness, swimming pool dress code).
14. **Cross-Service Recommendations**:
    - Link to Event Rentals (Barbeque grill, sound system rental, chairs/tents for private villa parties).
    - Link to Event Services (Decoration for birthday celebrations at resorts, Welcome Girls / Catering for private destination events).
15. **Footer & Sticky Mobile Action Bar**:
    - Complete footer with office address, contacts, and legal links.
    - Mobile bottom sticky action bar: "Call +91 96262 72817" & "WhatsApp Availability".
16. **SEO & Structured Data**:
    - LodgingBusiness & Resort Schema with price range and rating.
    - FAQPage Schema.
    - OpenGraph & Twitter tags.
