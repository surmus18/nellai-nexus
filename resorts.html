Frequently Asked Questions

What are the standard check-in and check-out timings?

Standard check-in time is 12:00 PM and check-out time is 11:00 AM. Early check-in or late check-out is subject to room availability and prior arrangement with the caretaker.

Are food arrangements available at the cottages and villas?

Yes! Most of our properties have in-house resident cooks who prepare authentic hot South Indian breakfasts, traditional meals, and evening BBQ snacks upon pre-order. Self-cooking kitchens with gas stoves and refrigerators are also available in selected villas.

Are bachelor groups and birthday party get-togethers allowed?

Yes, we have designated private standalone pool villas and estate properties (especially in Courtallam, ECR, and Kodaikanal) that welcome bachelor groups and family reunions. Moderate music and private campfire are permitted within legal eco-zone norms.

How do I reach the property and collect keys?

Once your booking is confirmed, you receive an official WhatsApp confirmation containing the exact Google Maps location pin, directions, and the mobile number of the dedicated property manager/caretaker who will welcome you at the gate.

Are pets allowed at the stays?

Many of our farmhouses and individual cottages in Courtallam, Ooty, and Yercaud are pet-friendly with secure open lawns. Please mention your pet details in the enquiry form so we recommend the ideal pet-welcoming property.
